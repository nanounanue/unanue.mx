Positions

Escuela de Gobierno y Transformación Pública, Tecnológico de Monterrey — 2020 to present

Director of AI Research; previously Associate Dean. Founded the Centro de Ciencia de Datos e IA and the data science center that carries ML work from pilot to production, including its governance, explainability and monitoring. Projects have covered case management and domestic violence risk for the Zacatecas prosecutor’s office, pharmaceutical logistics, port and terminal operations, school siting in El Salvador, and nationwide 5G planning.

Independent consulting — 2025 to present

Fractional CTO work for AI startups, machine learning advisory for early-stage ventures, and legal operations automation with language models and agentic workflows.

DSaPP, University of Chicago, and DSSG at Carnegie Mellon and the Alan Turing Institute — 2016 to 2022

Lead and senior machine learning researcher, and engineering manager. Led or contributed to twenty data science projects across six countries: emergency medical services placement, school dropout prevention, diabetes screening, HIV care retention, homelessness prevention, housing safety, inspection prioritization, police early warning, suicide lifeline routing, pollution enforcement, urban mobility and transport equity. Co-created Aequitas, a fairness auditing toolkit, and Triage, a framework for developing and operating ML models — both since adopted by government and nonprofit programs.

ITAM — 2019 to 2020

Director of the Centro de Ciencia de Datos and of the MSc in Data Science, the first such program in Latin America.

Open Intelligence, Datank and Sm4rt Predictive — 2011 to 2017

Co-founder and CTO, chief of machine intelligence, and lead data scientist across three ventures. Built production data products adopted by agencies and consultancies, and stood up a bank’s first fraud data warehouse, which cut detection time from weeks to under one week and reduced the rule set by roughly ninety percent.
