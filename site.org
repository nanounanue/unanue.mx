I work where optimization meets public infrastructure: ports, hospital
supply chains, electoral networks. Every objective function in those
systems encodes a political choice about who waits and who is served
first; my work is to make that choice explicit, measurable, and open
to argument.

** Toward Reducing Unproductive Container Moves: Predicting Service Requirements and Dwell Times
:PROPERTIES:
:EXPORT_FILE_NAME: container-moves
:EXPORT_DATE: 2026-04-06
:EXPORT_HUGO_CUSTOM_FRONT_MATTER: :venue "arXiv" :status "preprint" :arxiv "2604.06251" :pdf "https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.06251"
:END:

Two models over a container terminal's operational data: which
containers will request pre-clearance service, and how long each will
dwell in the yard. Evaluated with temporal validation against the
terminal's operating rules; the paper estimates a reduction of 13 % to
23 % of total handling operations [cite:@villalobos2026container].
