* Home
:PROPERTIES:
:EXPORT_HUGO_SECTION: /
:EXPORT_FILE_NAME: _index
:END:

Data science, machine learning, and operations research on
national-scale infrastructure.

I build decision systems for port terminals, hospital supply chains,
and electoral networks — and write about what they optimize. The work
has one through-line: *objective functions encode political choices*.
A model that allocates scarce capacity decides who waits; that
decision deserves daylight, not a footnote.

* About
:PROPERTIES:
:EXPORT_HUGO_SECTION: about
:EXPORT_FILE_NAME: _index
:END:

I work where optimization meets public infrastructure: ports, hospital
supply chains, electoral networks. Every objective function in those
systems encodes a political choice about who waits and who is served
first; my work is to make that choice explicit, measurable, and open
to argument.
