** Fewer Unproductive Moves at a Container Terminal
:PROPERTIES:
:EXPORT_FILE_NAME: container-terminal
:EXPORT_DATE: 2026-04-06
:END:

Most yard moves at a container terminal earn nothing: containers are
dug out, re-stacked, and shuffled aside to reach others. At the
terminal in the study, three of every four movements were
unproductive, and about half of those traced to pre-clearance service
requests — the administrative /previo/ a customs broker asks for,
distinct from formal customs inspection — that arrive with little
warning [cite:@villalobos2026container].

** Positioning Medicine Across a National Hospital Network
:PROPERTIES:
:EXPORT_FILE_NAME: hospital-network
:EXPORT_DATE: 2026-06-30
:END:

A public health system of national coverage — unnamed by agreement —
runs sixty-four warehouses that supply 1,456 medical units. Critical
medicines stocked out while others expired on the shelf: the network
held inventory, but in the wrong places, and first-expired-first-out
losses compounded the misallocation.

  <p>Inventory positioned by local judgment, shelf by shelf, across a network too large for judgment: shortages of critical medicines in some units coexisted with expiry losses in others.</p>
  <div class="label kicker">Scale</div>
  <p>Roughly 120 million pieces move through the network each month, across warehouses, the medical units they serve, and the supply points between them. At that size the policy has to be computed, not negotiated.</p>
  <div class="label kicker">Method</div>
  <p>Multi-echelon (s,S) and (R,S) inventory policies set by a stochastic program, with perishables handled first-expired-first-out. A digital twin replayed historical demand against every candidate policy, so service levels were measured before anything touched the field.</p>
  <div class="label kicker">Result</div>
  <p>The twin measured the service-level and inventory changes for each candidate policy; those figures are with the client for release review, and this page will carry them when they clear. What is public now is the method and the scale it ran at.</p>
