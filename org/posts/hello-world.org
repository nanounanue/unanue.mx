This site is built from Org-mode sources published through a custom
Tufte-styled HTML backend. Footnotes in the source[fn:1] are rewritten
at export time into Tufte-style sidenotes that appear in the wide right
margin on desktop, and as expandable notes on mobile.

The pipeline is intentionally minimal: =emacs --batch= loads
[[file:../publish.el][=publish.el=]], which defines an =org-publish-project-alist= and a
derived HTML backend that overrides the =footnote-reference=
transcoder. No Quarto, no Node, no Hugo.[fn:2]

* Why Tufte?

Edward Tufte's design language — wide margins for sidenotes and
figures, ET Book serif, generous line height — is particularly well
suited to long-form technical writing where references, caveats, and
asides want to live next to the main text instead of interrupting it.
