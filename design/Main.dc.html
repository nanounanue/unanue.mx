Data science, machine learning, and operations research on national-scale infrastructure.

I build decision systems for port terminals, hospital supply chains, and electoral networks — and write about what they optimize. The work has one through-line: objective functions encode political choices. A model that allocates scarce capacity decides who waits; that decision deserves daylight, not a footnote.
